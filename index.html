❓ FAQ

Do I need to do the topics in order?

Mostly yes. Fundamentals and Prompt Engineering are the base everything builds on. Handoff, Feedback Loops, and Orchestration assume you already understand how a single agent behaves and how to evaluate it. The colored dots show the rough difficulty climb.

Where do I actually learn each topic?

Inside every topic there's a "Where to study" block with one-click YouTube and Google searches (these stay current automatically) plus a short list of free resources — official docs, guides, and open-source tools. Open a topic and scroll to it.

How is my progress saved, and can I share this?

Yes — share the link freely. Progress is stored in each visitor's own browser via localStorage, so everyone who opens the page keeps their own checkmarks and quiz results on their own device. No account, no server. Clearing browser data or hitting "Reset progress" wipes it.

How do the quizzes work?

Each topic has a "Take Quiz" button that opens a short quiz. Click an option to lock in an answer. After the last question you'll see your score — you need 80% to pass. Hit ↻ Retry to try again as many times as you want.

Do I need a specific model or API?

No. The concepts apply to any LLM-based agent. To try things hands-on, any chat model with an API works — or mock the responses if you just want to practice the architecture.
